Shared money, together

Keep your own money apart and share a space with the people close to you. Invite them through iCloud: everyone adds operations, and each one shows whose coin it is.

  • Your space and a shared one, a tap apart
  • Invite through iCloud, no new sign-ups
  • Premium Family: up to 6 people, one subscription

Just say it

Type or say “coffee 4.50, taxi 12 and groceries 38.20”: three expenses appear, each in its category. Check them and add them in one go.

  • One sentence becomes several expenses
  • A keypad with a calculator built in
  • Receipts read on the phone, line by line

Every currency, one total

Cards, cash and savings, each in its own currency, added up in your main one at daily rates. A transfer between currencies is one operation at the rate you actually got.

  • 159 currencies, daily rates built in
  • Transfers at your real rate
  • Savings goals that are real accounts

What the trip really cost

Set the dates and the spending inside them joins the trip: the total in the local currency and in yours, per day, by category or day by day.

  • The total in two currencies
  • What a day costs you there
  • Categories or the whole feed of the trip

See where it goes

Spending by category and by day, your balance ahead with the payments coming up, and insights that tell you whether this month is a normal one.

  • Week, month and year
  • Monthly budgets per category
  • Insights and a forecast to the month's end

Who owes whom

Money you lent and money you borrowed, in any currency: what is left, by when, and every payment back.

  • Owed to me and I owe, side by side
  • Paid back in parts, in any currency
  • Settled debts kept out of the way
