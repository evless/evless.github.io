Write to evless56@gmail.com — a person reads every message and replies within a few days. Tell us your iPhone model, the app version (at the bottom of Settings) and what happened.

Restore Premium

Settings → Obolos Premium → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought the subscription with.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple and cancelled in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the period ends. Refunds are handled by Apple.

A new iPhone

Your records are kept on the iPhone and in your own iCloud. On the new phone, sign in with the same Apple ID, keep iCloud turned on for Obolos (iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud) and open the app — the records come back from iCloud.

Bank statements

Obolos never connects to your bank. To bring in what you missed, use Reconcile with statement and pick a PDF, CSV or XLSX statement from your bank app: only the transactions that are not in Obolos yet are added.

Export and deletion

Settings → Export saves your records as CSV or JSON. Settings → Delete account erases everything for good: the records on the phone, their copy in your iCloud and the account on our server. More in the Privacy Policy.
